# Catalogue Taxonomy and Product Data

## 1. Taxonomy principles

- Use a hierarchical `parent_id` category model rather than fixed category/subcategory columns.
- Support at least three public levels: department, category and product type.
- Every category has English/Arabic names, slugs, description, image, SEO fields, sort order and active status.
- A product has one primary category path and may have additional category mappings.
- Brands, attributes, concerns and collections are separate dimensions.
- Empty or inactive categories are hidden publicly.
- Deactivation is reversible; historical order references are preserved.

- New Arrivals — وصل حديثاً

## 5. Product master fields

### Identity

- Internal product ID/UUID.
- Vendor ID.
- English and Arabic name.
- English and Arabic slug.
- Brand.
- Primary category and additional category mappings.
- Product type.
- Status and publication window.
- Internal supplier/reference fields, permission-restricted.

### Commerce

- Base SKU or style code.
- Barcode/GTIN where available.
- Cost price, restricted by permission.
- List price and sale price in KWD.
- Sale start/end.
- Purchase limit.
- Return eligibility and policy class.
- Product weight and shipping dimensions.

### Content

- Short and full description in both languages.
- Key benefits.
- Ingredients.
- How to use.
- Warnings and storage instructions.
- Country of origin.
- Manufacturer/distributor where required.
- SEO title, description and share image per locale.
- Search keywords and synonyms per locale.

### Media

- Primary image.
- Gallery images.
- Optional video.
- Variant-specific images.
- English/Arabic alt text where context differs.
- Focal point and sort order.

### Inventory and lifecycle

- Track inventory toggle.
- Low-stock threshold.
- Batch/expiry tracking toggle.
- Preorder/backorder disabled by default.
- Shelf-life/expiry display policy.
- Discontinued/archived reason.

## 6. Variant fields

- Variant UUID.
- Product ID and vendor ID.
- Unique SKU.
- Barcode.
- Option combination, such as Shade: Rose and Size: 5 ml.
- Shade swatch value/image.
- Price override and sale-price override.
- Weight/dimension override.
- Primary media override.
- Track-stock toggle.
- Active/sellable status.
- Sort order.

## 7. Publishing validation

A product cannot be published unless it has:

- Required English and Arabic identity/content according to the locale policy.
- Active brand and primary category.
- At least one active sellable variant.
- Valid non-negative price.
- Unique SKU for every sellable variant.
- At least one approved image with alt text.
- Required usage/warning fields for its product type.
- No unresolved import or moderation error.

Stock can be zero at publication if “show out of stock” is enabled, but purchase remains disabled.

## 8. CSV import minimum columns

- External row key.
- Product name English/Arabic.
- Brand.
- Category path English or stable category code.
- SKU.
- Barcode optional.
- Option names/values.
- Price and optional sale price.
- Opening stock.
- Track batch/expiry flag.
- Image URLs only from approved/importable sources.
- Publish status.

The import process must preview changes, reject duplicate SKUs, report row-level errors and avoid partially duplicating a retried file.
